# Brainstorm

You are helping Taylor choose the next chunks of work. This is a short session for when the phase list feels thin, or when he is away from the desk. It is not a step on the delivery pipeline.

The rules for a phase, an epic, and a ticket are [`docs/PRODUCT.md`](../../docs/PRODUCT.md). Follow that file. Do not invent a second definition.

Also read:

- [`docs/PHASES.md`](../../docs/PHASES.md) — phases already accepted
- [`docs/FUTURE_FEATURES.md`](../../docs/FUTURE_FEATURES.md) — ideas that are not a phase
- [`docs/MVP_SPEC.md`](../../docs/MVP_SPEC.md) §4 — non-goals. A closed non-goal stays out unless Taylor reopens it in one line, with the reason.

## Talk, then wait

1. Name a few outcomes that close and are not already in `PHASES.md` or `FUTURE_FEATURES.md`. A second outcome is a separate idea. Do not add ideas to fill the list.
2. Ask what feels missing, and what would matter most if he played this week. Ask questions that can add an idea or drop one.
3. Stop. Wait for his answers.

## Sort, then wait

Put every idea he kept, plus the ones you proposed, into three notes: what should come first, what is an easy win, and what has the biggest impact. Give the reason for each placement. He can challenge the reason and change the order. Nothing in the sort is decided until he says so.

Stop again.

## Phases, then wait

Group only the ideas he wants built next into phases, in the shape `PRODUCT.md` describes. Show the player outcome, the epic titles, one sentence on each problem, what must already be true, and what stays later on purpose. Leave the rest as ideas.

Wait. A phase is written only after he accepts it.

## Write only what he accepts

- An accepted phase: add it to `PHASES.md`. Remove that idea from `FUTURE_FEATURES.md` if it was there.
- An idea that stays off the phase list: add it to `FUTURE_FEATURES.md` in the same shape as the entries already there.
- Do not file GitHub issues. Do not write tickets or acceptance criteria. Do not edit `QA_WORKFLOW.md`.

Then stop. [`plan-epic`](./plan-epic.md) is a different chat. It reads one phase, confirms the epics, and writes that epic's tickets after he reviews them.
